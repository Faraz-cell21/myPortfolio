04 · FAQ

Common questions

What do you do?

I'm a full stack developer with 2+ years of experience. I build web applications end to end: APIs, databases, authentication, real-time features, and the interfaces users interact with.

What is your primary tech stack?

On the backend I work heavily with Django, Node.js, and Express. On the frontend I use React with TypeScript, TanStack Router, TanStack Query, and Tailwind CSS.

For data I use PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, and Firebase depending on the project. See the Tools section for the full list.

What kinds of projects have you built?

Gaming platforms, compliance software, multi-tenant SaaS, real-time chat apps, feature flag systems, and admin dashboards. Check the Projects section above for details on each one.

Are you available for work?

Yes, I'm open to full-time roles, contract work, and freelance projects. Reach out via email or LinkedIn and we can discuss your timeline and requirements.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm comfortable working remotely with async communication, clear documentation, and regular check-ins to keep projects on track.

How do we get started on a project?

Send me a message with a short overview of what you need, your timeline, and any tech preferences. I'll reply with questions, a rough approach, and next steps for a kickoff call.
